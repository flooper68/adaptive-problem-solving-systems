The APS MVP is unfinished and has not run its complete loop — Verification

## Evidence

As of 2026-08-21 (grooming APS-S-41):

- The normative framework (`framework/` in the wiki: definition, declaration
  contract, vocabulary, visualization, changelog) still carries concepts and
  contracts introduced before any end-to-end loop showed which are necessary.
  12 of the 30 inventoried concepts have a recorded review; 18 review tasks
  remain open and none has run since the 2026-08-17 migration to Chaos House.
- Pre-migration self-use already removed duplicated surfaces (a separate plan,
  oversized logs); the maintainer's usability judgement was and remains the
  outcome evidence that matters.
- The first real need has now gone through the whole loop: maintainer
  feedback on a shallow grooming (APS-R-27) became a task (APS-60), an approved
  change to the grooming process, and a later grooming run (APS-S-40/S-41)
  that behaved differently because of it. This is one cycle, not yet a
  pattern.
- Teach-back by an uninformed reviewer and adversarial review have never run.
- Asked directly on 2026-08-21, the maintainer answered "no" to being able to
  explain and run the whole process unaided, and stumbled on grooming-process
  jargon ("revise" as a disposition name) in the same session.

Exact readings are in `records/problem-state-log.md`.

## Desired outcome

The smallest coherent APS MVP is explicitly bounded, nonessential concepts and
surfaces are removed or deferred, the retained pieces are finished and
consistent, and one real consumer need runs through the complete loop into an
approved adaptation that changes a later run.

## Signal

Read at any time, compared across sessions:

1. how many concepts in the review inventory have a recorded disposition and
   how many remain unreviewed;
2. which loop responsibilities — grooming, verification, compilation,
   adaptation, delivery — have run at least one real invocation and which
   never have; and
3. the maintainer's current answer to whether they can explain and run the
   whole process unaided, and where they last stumbled.

Movement toward the desired outcome shows as the unreviewed count falling,
never-run responsibilities becoming run, and the maintainer's answer
improving; readings may be qualitative. The maintainer's direct feedback is
the initial outcome verification.
