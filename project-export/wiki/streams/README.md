# Stewardship evidence streams

The system retains raw evidence where it originates when practical and keeps a durable summary or reference when the source is external. Chaos House records, tasks, problems, sessions, wiki events, and artifacts carry operational provenance; R54 supplies an offsite one-way projection rather than a second evidence store.

Problem histories preserve long-running condition decisions. Tasks preserve bounded work state. Working-session records preserve material interaction history. `insights` and `framework-feedback` retain their source claims and dispositions through intake and grooming. `framework-usage` and `external-foundations` retain recoverable references and syntheses appropriate to their sources.

Artifact checks, consumer trials, outcome observations, and delayed evidence stay linked to the relevant task, problem, session, or stream with enough context to repeat or challenge the conclusion.
