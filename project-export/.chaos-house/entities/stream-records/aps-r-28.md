Maintainer feedback (2026-08-21) on how APS reads and feels in use, from the perspective of a first-time user:

1. Jargon is too specific and confusing for first-time users. Several APS terms are hard to understand without already knowing the framework, so a newcomer cannot follow along or apply it without a glossary lookup at every step.

2. The agent operating the framework tends to use complex language and is "too structured" — it mirrors the framework's formal vocabulary and step structure back at the user instead of talking plainly. The result is that sessions do not feel nice or natural; the structure gets in the way of the work rather than supporting it.

Open questions for grooming: whether this is a framework-text problem (VOCABULARY / process documents using unnecessarily specialised terms that could be replaced with plain words), an agent-guidance problem (process documents and session types prescribing rigid, formal interaction that the agent follows too literally), or both. A useful check would be to read a recent session transcript as a newcomer and note every term or step that needed explaining.
