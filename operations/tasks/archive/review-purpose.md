# Review the purpose concept

## Intended result

Decide whether purpose is an independently useful APS concept or should be
merged with or demoted beneath the system problem or another concept.

## Current state

Superseded by the
[system-concept review](../../streams/working-sessions/2026-07-11-system-concept-review.md).
Its 2026-07-12 declaration field audit ruled that `purpose` restates the
problem, vision, name, and prose purpose, and the minimal declaration compiled
that day removed the field. Vision — purpose's sibling in the same ruling — had
its review task superseded and archived in that session; this task was missed
by the same sweep and remained open with nothing left to decide.

The [purpose review session](../../streams/working-sessions/2026-08-06-purpose-review.md)
verified on 2026-08-06 that no conceptual residue remains: the vocabulary never
defined a `Purpose` entry, and every surviving occurrence is a stream `purpose`
property (owned by `apss.review-information-stream`), a descriptive heading, or
ordinary prose.
